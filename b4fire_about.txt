About Us

B4Fire Technology – is a fire security, installation, maintenance and repairs company in South Africa.

B4Fire Technology was established in 2018. The aim is to become the number one Maintenance and Installation Company in the Fire and Security Market. Through numerous market studies, we can now say that we are one of the best when it comes to the delivery of quality work and customer satisfaction. One of our policies is to encourage staff through growth and to ensure that the highest standards are always maintained.
