Services

Services We Offer

B4Fire Technology have been involved in a variety of systems, with various combinations of the following:

Fire Detection

A fire detection system aims to monitor the presence of fire, to alert and allow control actions to be initiated manually or automatically. We install various types of fire detection addressable, conventional and standalone.

Gas Fire Suppression Systems

Gas Fire Suppression Systems are used to extinguish electrical fires by releasing a concentration of gas within a particular room. We install various types of Gas Fire Suppression Systems.

Access Control

Access control identifies users by verifying various login credentials, which can include usernames and passwords, PINs, biometric scans, and security tokens. Many access control systems also include multifactor authentication (MFA), a method that requires multiple authentication methods to verify a user’s identity.

Fire Stopping

Fire stopping helps buildings maintain their structural integrity and slow the spread of fire within a building.

Electric Fence

An electric fence is a barrier that uses electric shocks to deter people and/or other animals from crossing a boundary.

Fire Equipment

Firefighting equipment is a range of tools and appliances, building and office  that are designed to extinguish fires. When used correctly, this equipment will protect the operator from harm.

Evacuation and public address

Evacuation and public address systems allow for safe and controlled building evacuation to be managed in the case of an emergency. Clear voice messages can be initiated from the system to aid in building evacuation

Alarms

Designed to detect when something is amiss, deter the potential threat, and alert the proper authorities so they can then respond quickly and efficiently.  Alarms can also detect fire, carbon monoxide leaks, and/or environmental hazards. Alarms can be installed in your home or business

Boom gates

Boom gates are not just for security; they also facilitate the smooth flow of traffic. In busy environments such as parking lots, toll booths, and gated communities, boom gates regulate vehicle entry and exit, minimising congestion and ensuring orderly movement.

CCTV

CCTV systems are typically used to monitor and record the activity of a building or home , as well as its surroundings. These systems are used to primarily combat crime such as theft or vandalism. Also, these systems are also referred to as video surveillance

Fire doors

Fire doors are an integral part of a building’s passive fireproofing plan. They’re used for fire compartmentation – a process that places physical barriers in strategic areas around a building, in order to slow the spread of flames and smoke in the event of a fire.

Signage

They serve several common purposes: to promote, identify, provide information, give directions or to raise safety awareness.

First aid

First aid can include cleaning minor cuts, scrapes, or scratches; treating a minor burn; applying bandages and dressings; the use of non-prescription medicine; draining blisters; removing debris from the eyes; massage; and drinking fluids to relieve heat stress.

PPE

Personal protective equipment, commonly referred to as “PPE”, is equipment worn to minimize exposure to hazards that cause serious workplace injuries and illnesses.
