 Services We Offer

 B4Fire Technology have been involved in a variety of systems, with various combinations of the following: 

 Fire Detection

 A fire detection system aims to monitor the presence of fire, to alert and allow control actions to be initiated manually or automatically. We install various types of fire detection addressable, conventional and standalone.

 Gas Fire Suppression Systems 

 Gas Fire Suppression Systems are used to extinguish electrical fires by releasing a concentration of gas within a particular room. We install various types of Gas Fire Suppression Systems.

 Access Control

 Access control identifies users by verifying various login credentials, which can include usernames and passwords, PINs, biometric scans, and security tokens. Many access control systems also include multifactor authentication (MFA), a method that requires multiple authentication methods to verify a user’s identity.

 Fire Stopping

 Fire stopping helps buildings maintain their structural integrity and slow the spread of fire within a building.

 Electric Fence

 An electric fence is a barrier that uses electric shocks to deter people and/or other animals from crossing a boundary.

 Fire Equipment

 Firefighting equipment is a range of tools and appliances, building and office  that are designed to extinguish fires. When used correctly, this equipment will protect the operator from harm.
